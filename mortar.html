The Product

What is
Mortar?

01
Project Management Track every phase of a construction project from groundwork to handover.
02
Team Coordination Assign tasks, track crews, and keep every contractor in sync.
03
Site Documentation Logs, reports, and records — all in one place, always up to date.
04
Budget Tracking Real-time cost visibility so nothing slips past the numbers.

Construction is one of the last industries still running on spreadsheets, phone calls, and paper. Mortar is the fix — a focused, fast, and practical management tool built specifically for the realities of building.

From small contractors to larger site operations, Mortar brings together project timelines, team assignments, material tracking, and client communication into a single platform that actually works the way construction does.

The design is deliberately clean and direct — because the people using it are on sites, not at desks. Every feature earns its place. Nothing is there for show.
