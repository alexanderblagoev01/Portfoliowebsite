01  — The Brief Car Care / Bulgaria
One frame that survives a growing product line.
My Role
Label Designer
Output
Print-Ready Labels
Products
9 Label Variants
Format
150 ml Spray Bottles

Easy Car Cleaner is a Bulgarian car cosmetics brand making spray products for car care — from interior fragrances to glass de-icers and insect removers. It was adding products faster than it could design for them, and every label had been drawn from scratch.

The task was to fix the frame once — wordmark, artwork panel, copy block, category and volume — so a new product becomes an artwork swap, not a redesign. Each label wraps a matte black spray bottle; the artwork panel tells the products apart at a glance: bold gradients for the fragrances, close-up photography for the cleaners.

The result is a product family with real shelf presence: each item known by its colour and artwork, the range still reading as one brand.

Label
Anatomy

Five fixed zones, and only one of them changes: the artwork panel, which carries the product's name and colour.

  1. 01WordmarkStacked in three lines, in the same place on every label.
  2. 02Brand badgeThe car mark, top right, at a fixed size.
  3. 03Artwork panelThe only zone that changes: a gradient or a photograph, with the product's name.
  4. 04Copy blockThe Bulgarian description, ragged right on a fixed measure, so its length can vary.
  5. 05Category & volumeWhat it is on the left, how much on the right, along the bottom.
