Soooo… What’s Olli?

Olli is a meal subscription service built around the idea that cooking should feel like a choice, not a chore. Users browse a curated selection of recipes, pick what they want to make that week, and the exact ingredients arrive at their door — nothing more, nothing wasted.

The app goes beyond delivery. Each recipe comes with a step-by-step cooking tutorial built directly into the interface — timed, visual, and designed to guide you through the process without ever leaving the app.

The design challenge was building something that felt warm and approachable without losing the clean confidence of a premium product. The result is an interface that respects your time and makes the kitchen feel less intimidating.

Audit

Building a design system is the fastest way to test it. Two problems surfaced that a static file would never have shown.

01

A button label that couldn't fit its button

The symbol sheet draws every button at 246 × 68 with a 32pt label, but every real one in the flow is 40–44pt tall. Fixed with a proportional size ramp that keeps the spec for reference.

02

Two palettes in one file

The Colors page documents Avocado as primary, but the button symbols still painted an earlier purple and mint palette across 32 instances. The build follows the documented palette, and the old values are flagged.

02  — The App6 screens

From stranger to first box.

The path a new user takes, in the SwiftUI build. Each step asks one thing and shows how far along you are. Scroll, and the phone taps through it.

  1. 01

    Allergens

    Onboarding starts with what you can't eat: tap your allergens once and they're kept out of every week.

  2. 02

    Preferences

    Then how you like to eat — Meat & Veggies, Family Friendly, Fit & Wholesome, Veggie, Pescatarian, Quick and Easy. The feed leads with what you pick.

  3. 03

    Plan size

    People and recipes per week, each with a − and a +. Servings, price per serving and the weekly total update as you go.

  4. 04

    Checkout

    No surprises: the receipt shows what you paid and exactly when the box arrives.

  5. 05

    Your kitchen

    Home greets you by name, says when the box lands, and leads with this week's top picks and recipes chosen for your profile.

  6. 06

    Account

    Your plan, your orders and the app's appearance — system, light or dark — on one screen, behind iOS's own Liquid Glass tab bar.
