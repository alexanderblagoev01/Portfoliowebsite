Cooking

Ingredients

Olli is a meal subscription service built around the idea that cooking should feel like a choice, not a chore. Users browse a curated selection of recipes, pick what they want to make that week, and the exact ingredients arrive at their door — nothing more, nothing wasted.

The app goes beyond delivery. Each recipe comes with a step-by-step cooking tutorial built directly into the interface — timed, visual, and designed to guide you through the process without ever leaving the app.

The design challenge was building something that felt warm and approachable without losing the clean confidence of a premium product. The result is an interface that respects your time and makes the kitchen feel less intimidating.

02  — Onboarding
— Feature 01

Built around
you.

Olli's onboarding sets up a profile that actually matters. Select your allergens, dietary preferences — Meat & Veggies, Fit & Wholesome, Veggie, Pescatarian — and the app filters your weekly selection accordingly. The whole setup takes under two minutes.

03  — Recipe Discovery
— Feature 02

Browse,
pick, cook.

Choose how many people you're cooking for and how many recipes you want per week — the pricing updates in real time. Then pick from a curated grid of meals, each with a photo, cook time, and "Hall of Fame" or "New" tags to help you decide. You only see recipes that match your profile.

04  — The Kitchen
— Feature 03

Your box,
your terms.

The home feed greets you by name, shows your next delivery, and surfaces Top Picks alongside your profile-matched recipes. The account screen keeps your plan details, order history, and appearance settings all in one clean view — no buried menus, no hunting around.
